About Us

Equipping communities with life-saving AEDs and the confidence to use them.

Training company first. AED partner always.

HeartSafe Singapore exists because surviving a cardiac arrest shouldn't depend on luck. We bring together the two things that actually change the outcome: people who know what to do, and a defibrillator within reach.

We supply and maintain Mindray BeneHeart AEDs, approved for Singapore use, and we train the teams around them in CPR, AED response and choking first aid. When both are in place, we certify your establishment HeartSafe Ready.

One partner. End-to-end. From the wall cabinet to the confidence of the person standing in front of it.

Equip. Educate. Empower.

Equip

An AED within 3 minutes' reach can be the difference between life and loss. We put world-class Mindray devices where they're needed: installed, signed and maintained.

Educate

Machines don't save lives, people do. Our hands-on courses strip away the fear so that anyone, from receptionist to CEO, can respond in the first minutes.

Empower

HeartSafe Ready certification turns preparedness into pride: a growing community of establishments telling Singapore that a life is safe here.
