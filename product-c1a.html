How to use it, step by step

Based on the official Mindray BeneHeart quick reference guide. The AED talks you through every step.

Check and call

Confirm the person is unresponsive and not breathing normally. Shout for help, call 995 and send someone to fetch the AED.

Open the lid

The AED powers on automatically and starts guiding you with voice prompts. Stay calm and follow along.

Apply the pads

Expose the chest and stick on the pre-connected pads exactly as the diagrams show: one below the right collarbone, one on the left side. For young children, flip the child mode switch.

Deliver the shock

The AED analyses the heart rhythm by itself. Do not touch the patient. If a shock is advised, press the flashing shock button within 30 seconds, then continue CPR with the metronome until paramedics arrive.

If no shock is advised, perform CPR according to the prompts. This summary does not replace hands-on practice: book a training session and run a full rescue with our trainers.
