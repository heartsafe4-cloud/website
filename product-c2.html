Description

The BeneHeart C2 pairs everything the C1A does with a 7 inch TFT colour screen. Animated guidance mirrors every voice prompt, so rescuers in a noisy MRT station or a panicked office see exactly what to do next.

With the CPR sensor, the screen scores every compression in real time: depth, rate and recoil. It is the closest thing to having a trainer beside you during a real rescue.

Key features
  • 7″ TFT colour screen, 800 × 480, auto brightness
  • Animated rescue guidance mirroring every prompt
  • CPR real-time feedback on compression quality
  • QShock™ + ResQNavi™ smart coaching
  • 3 languages, one-switch adult / child mode
  • IP55 rated, 1.5 m drop tested
  • Voice recording up to 1 hour, CPR data up to 5 hours
