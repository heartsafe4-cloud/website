Choking First Response

Choking can turn fatal in minutes, and it happens where we least expect it: at lunch, at home, in the classroom. Learn to clear an airway safely for adults, children and infants.

Book This Course

Minutes matter. Know exactly what to do.

Perfect for F&B teams, schools, preschools, eldercare staff and parents. Short, practical and repeated hands-on until the technique sticks.

  • Recognise choking instantlyIncluding silent choking, when the casualty cannot speak or cough.
  • Back blows, done rightPosition, force and rhythm that actually dislodge an obstruction.
  • Abdominal thrustsSafe, effective technique for adults and children.
  • Infant-specific responseGentle, correct techniques for children under one year old.
  • When it becomes cardiac arrestHow to escalate to CPR and get help on the way.
